# DJConnect Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

DJConnect is a community project for people who care about music, Home
Assistant, local-first devices and useful software. We want this repository to
be welcoming, practical and safe for contributors with different backgrounds,
experience levels and perspectives.

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or other similar
personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that helps build a healthy community:

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback with care.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code and project outcomes, not people.
- Assume good intent where reasonable, and ask clarifying questions before
  escalating.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing secrets, tokens, personal data or private
  communications without permission.
- Be patient with new contributors and direct them to the relevant docs when
  possible.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling or demeaning comments.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately posting secrets, credentials, tokens or private artifacts.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional open-source community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all DJConnect project spaces, including GitHub
issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, release notes and any
community support spaces that clearly represent the DJConnect project.

It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public
spaces, for example by using an official project email address, posting through
an official project account, or acting as a maintainer.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it to the project
maintainer:

- Peter van Tol, via GitHub: `@pcvantol`

Please include as much relevant context as you safely can, such as links,
screenshots, dates, involved usernames and what outcome you are hoping for.
Reports will be handled with care and discretion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of
acceptable behavior. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code,
issues, pull requests or other contributions that do not align with this Code
of Conduct.

Depending on the situation, maintainers may take actions including:

- A private explanation or warning.
- A public clarification or moderation note.
- Temporary or permanent restrictions from project spaces.
- Reporting serious abuse to the relevant platform or authority when needed.

Enforcement will aim to be fair, proportionate and focused on protecting the
community.
