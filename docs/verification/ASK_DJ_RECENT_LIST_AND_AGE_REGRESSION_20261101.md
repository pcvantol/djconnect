Recent-played cards disappeared when their RAM overlay expired: the existing
Store omitted `items`, and clients fell back to the complete bullet text.
[`recent_history_presentation`](../product/ASK_DJ_RECENT_LIST_PRESENTATION_V1.md)
separates retained original display labels from temporary action/media rights.
Caption and cards can render once under the original source/owner checks.
It does not add Play Now or renew an action, artwork or speech lease.

“Hoe oud is Novastar” resolved to a public group page without a birth date.
The closed semantic read angle `age` now lets Core ask whether the listener
means band duration or a person's age. This requires an exact, current English
Wikipedia/QID direct group lead with bounded known descriptors. A documentary,
book, company or later mention of a band cannot qualify. English evidence can
produce the local clarification in all five UI languages. Unsupported
descriptors or missing/expired/non-English evidence remain ordinary assessed
research; this is an availability limit, not permission to guess a numeric age.

The clarification contains no invented age, date, member or role. Its original
source expiry remains fenced through final delivery. It is a Core-owned
disambiguation question, not a factual biography accepted without review.
Ordinary factual answers retain all existing semantic/date/style guards.
