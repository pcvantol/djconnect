## Result and boundary

Pi 4-inch is a compact shared-room native appliance: bounded playback,
current playback, queue/playlist interactions, compact Current DJMoment,
renderer-safe Track Insight and read-only structured Ask DJ actions. Full
Session Flow, personal rich-client authority, local voice/audio, local Session
ownership and Universal Receiver/browser substitution are intentional absences.

Target-hardware compact-projection and shared-profile visibility evidence remain
Future Assessment items. No Pi code, Runtime, Renderer, API, privacy-policy,
roadmap or Execution Horizon change is authorized.
