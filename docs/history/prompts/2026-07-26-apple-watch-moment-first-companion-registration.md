## Archived prompt

Register **Apple Watch Moment-First Conversational Companion** as a future
assessment-first Product Development experience within Phase 3 Apple Premium
Experience. Do not create a separate CMB number and do not alter the current
Execution Horizon.

The Watch is a moment-first, conversation-first Concrete Renderer Host: during
an active Session it opens on Current DJMoment, may later provide bounded active
Session Flow cards and a compact renderer-safe Session Projection, and keeps
Ask DJ Push to Talk prominent. With no active Session, Ask DJ PTT is its only
primary experience. It is not a Runtime/Session/Music Backend/Profile owner,
remote control, Now Playing clone, queue or playlist editor, history client,
configuration host or miniature iPhone app.

Record future Watch-local presentation only: native interaction, one-tap
Interactive DJMoment rendering, bounded semantic Audience Expressions and a
fragment-based Lyric Moment are potential candidates. They do not change
Planner, Knowledge, DJMoment Engine, Audience Signal, Performance Memory or
Lyrics Knowledge ownership. Raw emoji is never domain semantics; lyrics remain
behind the Deferred Lyrics Knowledge capability and cannot become a lyrics
viewer.

Session Continuation remains independently owned. The Watch may later present
a valid mirrored invitation, but must obtain/revalidate current Runtime state;
neither iPhone-local state nor WatchConnectivity is canonical. Default future
posture is iPhone-targeted APNs with platform mirroring where available, not a
new Watch push requirement. All Watch content is renderer-safe: never Music
DNA, Ask DJ history, credentials, tokens, Profile-management content, internal
Runtime identities, full queues or sensitive/stale notification content.

Future assessment may inspect existing watchOS evidence, Current DJMoment,
bounded Flow/Crown navigation, compact Projection, PTT/current answer,
active/inactive Session states, interaction presentation, continuation,
accessibility, battery/latency/network and privacy. Each delivery candidate is
separately assessed and authorized. The existing Apple companion is evidence,
not approval of its current UI as the future product target.
