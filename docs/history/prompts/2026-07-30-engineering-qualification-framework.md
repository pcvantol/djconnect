# Platform Engineering — Engineering Qualification Framework

**Status:** Implemented and finalized through PR #628
**Implementation merge:** `a59c07599496249d7e2109469c971dd1e7fa52d2`

## Objective

Make the local Engineering Platform self-qualifying through deterministic,
repeatable scenarios and local evidence.

## Delivered scope

- Canonical 19-capability Engineering Qualification Registry.
- `dj-engineer qualify` runner and local JSON/Markdown evidence dashboard.
- Scenario PASS/FAIL evidence with coverage, duration, diagnostics and version
  metadata.
- Latest qualification status in terminal Engineering Reports.
- Engineering Platform version 1.3.0.

## Boundaries preserved

Qualification is local engineering tooling only. It changes no Product, Runtime,
Release, Deployment or Engineering governance behavior.
