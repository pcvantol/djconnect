## Product and ownership

different new stories published within the last30minutes, and prepares one
60–110word source-grounded narrative in the current Session language, Mood and
Persona. Source text is untrusted data. Existing configured HA generation is the
only permitted model boundary; this feature introduces no provider, key or

### Metadata exceptions and generated-output notice

Explicit RSS channel/item or Atom feed/entry rights, copyright, licence or
licence-link declarations are not replaced by a publisher default. Such a feed
or entry is unavailable until its particular conditions are separately
qualified. Even empty or apparently permissive declarations fail closed; there
is no free-text permission classifier. A restricted item does not suppress its
otherwise eligible siblings, while an item cannot override restricted feed
conditions. No linked article is fetched to infer permission.

One narrowly reviewed channel declaration is admitted: the fixed KNMI RSS feed's
exact top-level, attribute-free, child-free copyright value
`Copyright: KNMI, https://www.knmi.nl/`. The official KNMI copyright page applies
CC0 to site content by default while preserving item exceptions; the existing
source/adaptation notice remains mandatory. This owner identification alone does
not create a new licence. Any changed/duplicate/namespaced/nested declaration,
additional feed restriction, or item-level rights declaration remains unavailable.
The same text on another publisher's feed is not admitted. Original publication
and monotonic expiry still apply; the actual observed KNMI items were too old,
so this source preparation generated no live bulletin.

The closed five-source policy follows the linked publisher defaults and their
exceptions. CBS/EEA require original-source acknowledgement and preserved
meaning; KNMI requests source credit and separation from derived work. NASA's
AI guidance separates factual source-material disclosure from authorship of
generated output. Eurostat requires edited/translated reuse to be identified
and disclaims responsibility for that derivative text. A NASA guidance label
is not a generic Creative Commons licence or blanket third-party permission.

The complete candidate therefore includes a deterministic notice in en/nl/de/fr/es:
DJConnect authors the AI adaptation; translation is included where applicable;
the two publishers are identified strictly as source material and have not
reviewed or endorsed this summary and are not responsible for its contents.
This conditional translation wording does not assert that every source was
translated. The fixed notice travels in the ordinary content field through
owner Flow, native delivery and speech, without an extra screen or setting.
Existing source cards retain original links, dates and default licence labels.

The generation word budget subtracts the exact localized notice. The final
complete text remains 60–110 words and at most 1200 Unicode characters. The
existing second model call assesses that complete text, including consistency
with the notice and absence of publisher-authored/approved AI-output framing;
no additional call is introduced. Every candidate eligibility check recomputes
the notice against the same closed publisher policy. A missing, changed or
stripped notice cannot become a deliverable candidate. This is a bounded
metadata-summary policy, not certification of rights absent from feed metadata.
Actual source/style and native/audio acceptance remain separate qualification.

## Intake and reservation semantics

The parser accepts RSS2 or Atom, explicit publication dates with timezone, and
HTTPS article links on each source's literal host allowlist. It rejects missing
publication dates, `updated`-only Atom entries, future dates and items at or
beyond publication+1800seconds. CBS's actual explicit `+02:00` spelling is
normalized without inventing a timezone. A304 does not renew original dates.
Intake captures a monotonic deadline before HTTP/lock waits. Builder retains
that deadline and its own original pre-await clock anchor through storage,
generation and assessment. Frozen/backwards UTC cannot grant a fresh lease
after work finishes. Storage quarantine is checked again inside its lock,
including queued preparation and candidate consumption.

HTTP is cancellable with an8second timeout per feed, no redirects, bounded
512KiB decompressed response, bounded XML depth/node count, no DTD/entities,
conditional ETag/Last-Modified and per-source exponential backoff up to1hour.
The lifecycle owner must provide a dedicated cookie-free, credential-free HTTP
session. The collector creates no tasks and cannot publish. At most64 metadata
items per source remain in RAM. Failed publishers do not stop other feeds.

Same URL and tracking-query/GUID changes cannot add a second item. The Builder
also compares normalized titles and conservative token/string similarity,
requires two different publishers, and separately asks the existing generation
owner to assess semantic independence. Two reports of one event are not two
stories. This assessment is not an infallible deterministic event classifier;
cross-language/rewording and unsupported-claim cases remain qualification gates.

Before the first model call, URL/title hashes are durably reserved for24hours
in a bounded256-key backend-internal store. No source text, generated narrative,
Profile or Session ID is written to that dedup store. Unexpired evidence is
never evicted to allow work. Generation failure, timeout, cancellation and
process restart do not release an attempted pair for automatic re-generation.
A lost RAM candidate means silence. A candidate retains the earliest original
publication+30minute deadline on both UTC and monotonic clocks. Reads never
renew it. Planner consumption is once-only; failed publication must not trigger
a second model generation or forced catch-up announcement.

Internal persistence migration13 adds hash/expiry attempts and a clock table;
it changes no Profile preference or client schema. Reservations use confirmed
transactions in the existing SQLite persistence service. Actual HA SDK inspection
showed that HA Store.async_save can swallow write failures or defer a shutdown
write, so it is not used as the model-admission proof. The normal provider's
commit/rollback and process-restart guarantees apply; no new physical power-loss
durability guarantee is claimed. Failed or corrupt storage quarantines both
preparation and consumption, including already-waiting tasks.

One HA-owned cancellable background task and one dedicated cookie-free HTTP
client are created after persistence bootstrap. The technical interval callback
is an HA coroutine job, not executor-thread work. Last-entry unload cancels
preparation and closes the client before persistence shuts down. Original
owner/source/entry/agent/settings authority is captured before awaits. Agent
identity and public no-tools metadata are rechecked across both model calls.
Background news clears inherited Ask DJ turn context and writes no Ask DJ turn.
A published Moment privately retains the same original throwing authority guard.
Display and speech recheck it synchronously, including service identity/unload;
revocation latches closed. Reads never reconstruct or revive admission. The
private guard is excluded from wire serialization and historical persistence.
