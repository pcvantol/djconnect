# DJConnect prompt initialization

The generated projection owns generic prompt initialization, bootstrap,
authorization, branch/worktree and evidence requirements. Use `BOOTSTRAP.md`
and `docs/ai-development/DJCONNECT_DEVELOPMENT_EXTENSION.md` for the local
entrypoint and additions.

DJConnect record handling distinguishes `MERGED_UNRECONCILED` and
`MERGED_RECONCILED`; a dedicated Finalization increment may reconcile the
former in the canonical rolling records.

## Golden Scenario Governance Check

A DJConnect Verification or Session Intelligence increment must identify the
approved Golden Scenario relationship it protects, executes, captures or
extends. When no direct relationship exists, retain explicit architectural
justification and proportionate narrower validation evidence. This is a
DJConnect product-qualification requirement, not a generic contract. It must
not introduce a duplicate Runtime, Scenario Driver or verification path.
