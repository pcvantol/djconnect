# DJConnect release notes

## 4.0.0

DJConnect brings a personal music DJ to Home Assistant. Use Marketplace to
install DJConnect, then add the integration under **Settings → Devices & services**.

- Use Ask DJ to request music, get recommendations, and hear DJ contributions.
- Choose Spotify Direct or Music Assistant as your music connection.
- Use Home Assistant Assist, pair a DJConnect app, or connect a supported local device.
- Share a DJ session on a VibeCast screen through the app.

Requires **Home Assistant 2026.11.1 or later**. Set up an Assist pipeline with
a conversation agent. For voice, configure speech-to-text and text-to-speech.
See [installation and setup](docs/user/installation.md).
