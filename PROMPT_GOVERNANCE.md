# DJConnect prompt governance

Generic prompt scope, authorization, lifecycle handoff and reviewability are
governed by the generated projection. DJConnect retains its prompt index,
immutable Prompt History and product/qualification record conventions in the
local development extension and their canonical local records.

The local record convention has one exception: only its dedicated Finalization
reconciliation may restore `MERGED_UNRECONCILED` to `MERGED_RECONCILED`.
